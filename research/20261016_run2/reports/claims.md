## Notes

- **Errors are fuzzy comparisons.** Review 1's 94% is its three bin shares rounded first and then added (0.36 + 0.32 + 0.26 = 0.94); unrounded, its own 2^22-input run (seed 5) gives 93.6%. The two old figures therefore agree. Review 1's '100% above 0.5' is the same rounding: accuracy is 99.95%.
- **Errors made at the last input.** Error start step (smallest t from 5 on after which the pointer stays wrong), share of errors: <=5: 18.2%, 6: 2.0%, 7: 3.8%, 8: 7.8%, 9: 17.7%, 10: 50.6%. Readouts before step 5 are unreliable, so earlier starts are lumped into '<=5'.
- **Top-k variance curve.** Without the noise correction the shares are higher by about (1 - share)/256: 0.041, 0.244, 0.628, 0.769, 0.837, 0.882, 0.909, 0.937, 0.965.
- **Sensitivity by weight block.** The old range 0.73-0.90 is one set of 12 draws; four sets of 12 give the ranges in row 6a, so quote the median and percentiles instead. Median drops per other block: W_hh into clean neurons from the 10 others (60) 0.0; W_hh into the 10 others from clean neurons (60) 1.1; W_hh among the 10 other neurons (100) 0.4; input weights of the clean neurons (6) 1.7; input weights of the 10 others (10) 0.0; readout weights from clean neurons (60) 2.0; readout weights from the 10 others (100) 0.2 points. Blocks that do not involve the clean neurons' input or outgoing weights never cost more than 1.9 points.
- **Five sensitive weights.** Review 1 also reported n2's self-weight x 0.98 giving accuracy 0.80 when the max is 9 steps old and 0.93 when it just arrived; this run gives 0.801 and 0.935.
- **Noisy copies fail by position.** Across all 96 noisy copies the share explained by the top-3 positions has median 0.20 and range 0.03-0.61 (SE per copy at most 0.0005); its correlation with true accuracy is -0.85. Naive (uncorrected) shares in the five review copies: mult0.005_5 0.057, mult0.01_3 0.425, mult0.01_8 0.103, mult0.02_8 0.610, mult0.02_16 0.393; base 0.028; the noise correction is below 0.001 at 2^23 inputs. The mild copy mult0.005_5 (true accuracy 0.932) gives 0.057.
- **Per-cell estimator.** On the base model the estimator gives 0.939 (true 0.953).
- **Comparison thresholds by age.** Thresholds beyond about 0.85 or 1.15 lie outside the ratios used in the fit and are extrapolations. The unfiltered extremes are 1.73 (mult0.02_9, age 7, SE 0.24) and -0.18 (mult0.02_19, age 8, SE 0.38). mult0.02_19's thresholds by age 0-8: 0.96, 0.94, 0.92, 0.87, 0.88, 0.87, 0.77, 0.45, -0.18. The base model's thresholds by age: 1.022, 1.000, 1.011, 1.002, 1.004, 1.005, 0.988, 0.999, 0.988.
