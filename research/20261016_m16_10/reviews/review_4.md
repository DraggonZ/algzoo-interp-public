# Review 4: re-check of the claims added after review 3

**Bottom line.** Most new numbers trace correctly to their logs, but fix the following before posting. The M5,4 "resolution" (R10) does not hold for the code as maintained. Several ranges and cost statements are off. The drafts break two of the project's rules.

- **M5,4.** The quoted exact value 0.7456525 comes from the earlier quadrature. `table_exact_len4.log` was made at 18:26 with commit d097df5, and its values equal degree 7 everywhere. The maintained default puts a centroid rule on narrow pieces (commit bd3de8b, 19:19). It gives 0.7456351, 6.8 standard errors (SE) below all seven Monte Carlo runs pooled. With the same default, M6,4 is 3.9 SE low. The float32 explanation is also wrong. Review 3's two references were one float32 run and one float64 run, both high. The three float32 runs pooled agree with the degree-7 value.
- **Budgeted-mode ratios.** These were computed from log values rounded to two digits. The exact ranges are 0.12–0.33 (not 0.13–0.35), 0.40–1.0 (not 0.37–1.0), 0.18–0.33 without seed 3 (not 0.18–0.35) and 0.53–1.0 (not 0.49–1.0).
- **Costs.** R15's "more than 5×10^7" is false for 7 of the 16 length-5 runs it covers. The draft's "about 150× per extra input" is wrong from length 4 to 5. "ARC's 1,000–100,000 window" is the note's window: ARC's post names no range. The counter also skips each piece's Gram matrix, determinant and SVD, roughly +15–20% at full enumeration. That work is ordinary arithmetic, not qhull.
- **Drafts.** The qhull caveat is missing next to two costs and in the tweet. "Meets your criterion" needs its budget limit in the same sentence.
- **Near-tie pilot.** More than half of the 95% agreement is cases where neither method finds a band. The post-hoc rule removes cases where the scan does find a real band. Its "34–160×" is per σ, while the rows it is compared with are pooled.

Checks: [review_4_checks/](review_4_checks/) (`len4_quadrature.log`, `len5_default.log`, `misc_checks.log`).

## Confirmed

Three float64 M5,4 runs pool to 0.7456507 ± 3.8e-6 (z +0.5 against degree 7). The M16,4 best-of-five file is identical to zoo seed 3. Length-5 agreement holds for nine of ten models (\|z\| ≤ 1.31), and with the maintained default for the five rechecked (\|z\| ≤ 1.15). M16,5 is 0.99250522 against 0.99250436 ± 9.3e-7. The six noisy M16,5 copies have z from −1.36 to +1.25. The M2,5 bracket is 0.305164–0.305225: it holds the Monte Carlo value 0.305174, and its width equals the lost 6.2e-5. The recounted costs (909; 123,176; 141,588; 237,792) and the adjacency term (k·m·pairs) are in `exact_nd.py`. R17 per-σ exact RMSEs are 2.1, 3.3 and 5.0e-6, against sampling's 2.1, 3.2 and 4.5e-4. Seed costs and errors at both thresholds match the logs. Pooled copy ratios are 0.080, 0.443, 1.678 and 6.098 at 74,212, 60,134, 47,199 and 34,720 samples. Budgeted mode overestimates on every copy and every seed. Near-tie seed errors are −0.8, −13.0, −12.4, −1.9, −3.5 and −4.4, or 12.9–28.9 without the rule. Copy RMSEs are 0.0145, 0.0283 and 0.0535, costs 7,609–13,375, and 888 × 8 = 7,104 cases. The tweet has 256 characters and puts "Length 4" first. The LW draft puts "exact computation, not an explanation" in the same sentence as "meets your criterion", and calls seeds primary and copies a per-σ diagnostic.
