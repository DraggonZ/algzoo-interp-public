Net bits are its fair count: absolute reference class, one spread per kind, best of KL shares 0.001, 0.002 and 0.005 for the constants. It charges each stated spread (4 row spreads, one per kind of constant) at 6 bits, plus the rule's declared structure.

### Round 2 note (branch r2b)

- Best of branch: `rules/r2b_shared_store.py`, **−6 bits** (+12 over r1b_masked): one store coefficient for all four hiding neurons.
- The previous-jump term of 6 and 8 is not a correction for switched-off neurons (clipping is real). It stands in for what the 2x2 store/detector recursion misses: with ARC's actual store and detector the jump term is unneeded and neurons 6 and 8 fit much better. The same obstacle blocks building the store or the late gate from the hiding neurons with ARC's weights: their hiding inputs are cancelled by memory-neuron inputs (14, 11, 10, 0). A better store probably needs a rule for the memory neurons.
- The fitted feedback from hiding neurons 1, 6 and 8 already equals ARC's own weights between hiding neurons (within about 0.03; `r2b_look.log`). The feedback from neuron 7 differs from ARC's by a roughly common +0.75 for all four readers. So 12 of the 16 feedback constants restate weights rather than explain them.
